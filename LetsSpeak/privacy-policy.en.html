LetsSpeak! - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung

LetsSpeak! ("the app") shows live how much each person speaks in a group discussion. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and what choices you have.

1. Who is responsible

Gkane (individual developer), Switzerland
E-mail: garbis.alexa@gmail.com

Swiss law applies. For users in the European Economic Area the GDPR applies as well, see section 15.

2. In short

  • Voice recognition runs entirely on your phone. Microphone audio is analysed in memory and is never stored and never sent anywhere.
  • The app stores a voice profile (a short list of numbers) for each person you enroll, plus names, colours, settings and the results of your discussions - only on your phone.
  • The free version shows ads (Google AdMob). Ads need an internet connection and involve data described in section 6. The paid premium version has no ads.

4. Voice profiles are sensitive data

A voice profile is derived from a person's voice and can be used to recognise that person. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) this is biometric data. We process it only with the explicit consent of the person whose voice it is (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR; in Switzerland, consent to the processing of sensitive personal data under the Federal Act on Data Protection, FADP), which that person gives by ticking the consent box for their name and taking part in the voice enrollment. If you enroll other people, you must make sure they agree first. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by deleting the voice profile in the app; this does not affect earlier use. The voice profile never leaves your phone and is not used for any other purpose.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone - to hear the discussion.
  • Foreground service (microphone) and notifications - to keep recording while the screen is off; a notification shows that the app is listening.
  • Vibration - for the optional warning when someone speaks very much.
  • Internet / network state - for ads (free version) and purchases only.
  • Advertising ID - used by the ads in the free version, see section 6.

6. Ads (free version)

The free version shows small banner ads and, after a discussion of at least one minute, at most every few minutes a full-screen ad that you can close, provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). The ad software (Google Mobile Ads SDK) may process data such as your advertising ID, app set ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, diagnostic information and how you interact with ads. This is used to show and measure ads, to limit how often they appear, to prevent fraud, and - if you allow it - to personalise ads. No ads are shown during a discussion.

We decided to include this software in the app and are therefore responsible, together with Google, for collecting these data in the app and passing them to Google. Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC process them further as independent controllers under their own terms (business.safety.google/privacy). The data may be transferred to Ireland or other EU countries and to the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The consent form shows the list of ad technology providers.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy options. In other regions the app may show ads according to local rules. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or buy premium to remove all ads. More information: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Legal basis: your consent (for users in the EEA: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

7. Purchases (premium)

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google processes your payment and account data under its own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that the purchase exists (a purchase token) so that ads can be turned off. We never see your payment details. Google Play shows us order information such as the order number, product, price, date and country, which we use only for support and accounting. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal record-keeping duties.

8. What we do not do

No user accounts, no analytics or tracking tools of our own, no crash-reporting service, no sale of data, no sharing of voice data with anyone.

9. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app (Settings > Delete all my data, or single saved voices and discussions) or uninstall the app. We hold no copy.

10. Security

The app keeps its data in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Backup of the app's data to the cloud is switched off. We run no server of our own. Ads and purchases are handled by Google's software, which transmits data over encrypted connections.

11. When you contact us

If you write to us, we use your e-mail address and your message only to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering requests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

12. Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, you can inspect and delete it yourself in the app. Under the GDPR you also have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to a data protection authority, for example the one of your country of residence. Contact: garbis.alexa@gmail.com.

In Switzerland the competent authority is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), edoeb.admin.ch.

13. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

14. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your data, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent again. The current version is always available at this address.

15. Applicable law

This policy is governed by Swiss law, in particular the Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Where the GDPR applies to you because you are in the European Economic Area, its rules apply in addition and your GDPR rights remain unaffected.
