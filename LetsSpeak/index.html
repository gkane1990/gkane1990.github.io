LetsSpeak!

See who really gets to speak.

LetsSpeak! measures the speaking time of everyone in a discussion (2 to 8 people) and shows it live as bars that always add up to 100%. Each person reads a short text once so the app knows their voice.

Voice recognition runs on your phone: microphone audio is never stored or sent anywhere. Android 8.0 or newer. The free version shows ads outside of discussions; a one-time purchase removes them.
